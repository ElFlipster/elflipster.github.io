Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

JARVIS is a private, personal tool used by one person (its owner) on their own computer. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What Google data it uses

  • The "JARVIS" calendar it creates in its owner's Google account: it creates, updates and removes events there, copied from its own calendar.
  • The owner's other calendars, read-only: the list of calendars and their events, to check for clashes and plan the day.

How the data is handled

  • Calendar data is stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • It is never sold, shared, transferred to anyone else, or used for advertising.
  • It is not used to train any AI model.
  • Sign-in details are kept in a private file on that computer and are never logged.
  • Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google Account under Security > Third-party apps with account access.

JARVIS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
