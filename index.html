JARVIS

A private personal assistant, built for and used by one person.

JARVIS runs on its owner's own computer. It helps with everyday organisation: turning quick notes into tasks and appointments, keeping a personal calendar, and reminders.

Google Calendar

  • JARVIS keeps its own calendar on its owner's computer and copies those events to a separate Google calendar called "JARVIS", so they reach the owner's phone and watch.
  • It can read the owner's other Google calendars, read-only, to avoid clashes.
  • It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else can sign in to it.
